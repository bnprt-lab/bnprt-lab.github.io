Cats vs Dogs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Cats vs Dogs (the “Game”) for iPhone, iPad and web portals processes. Questions: dynamo1994@gmail.com.

Data the Game itself stores

Your progress (coins, levels, campaign stars, skins, settings) is stored only on your device. We run no servers, require no account and do not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the device advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the iOS “Allow Tracking” prompt), IP address / approximate location, device and app interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google’s consent form; you can change your choice any time in the Game: Language → Ad privacy settings. If you decline tracking, you still get ads, just non-personalized ones. Details: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To verify purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase history and device information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Web portal versions

When you play on a web portal (for example via Playgama), the portal shows ads, processes payments and may collect usage statistics under its own privacy policy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads for younger players can be limited through Google’s consent tools.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data on our servers, deleting the Game deletes all its data. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above or contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Игра хранит прогресс только на вашем устройстве, аккаунт не нужен, своих серверов у нас нет. Реклама показывается через Google AdMob: при вашем согласии (запрос iOS «Разрешить отслеживание») используется рекламный идентификатор устройства, IP и данные об устройстве. Покупки обрабатывает Apple, проверку покупок — RevenueCat. Изменить согласие на рекламу: Язык → Настройки рекламы и приватности. Вопросы: dynamo1994@gmail.com.
