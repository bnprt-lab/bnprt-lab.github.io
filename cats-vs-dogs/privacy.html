Cats vs Dogs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Cats vs Dogs (the “Game”) for iPhone, iPad, Android and web portals processes. Questions: dynamo1994@gmail.com.

Data the Game itself stores

Your progress (coins, levels, campaign stars, skins, settings) is stored only on your device. We run no servers of our own and require no account. The Game does not ask for your name, contacts, photos or location. It asks for an e-mail address only if you choose to add one to a bug report (see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the device advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking in the iOS “Allow Tracking” prompt; on Android you can reset or delete it in the Android settings), IP address / approximate location, diagnostic data and app interaction data (for example ad views and taps) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google’s consent form; you can change your choice any time in the Game: Language → Ad privacy settings. If you decline, you still get ads, just non-personalized ones. Premium players see no ads. Details: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple, Google Play and RevenueCat)

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple on iPhone and iPad and by Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details. To verify purchases and unlock what you bought we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy. You manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Online battles

When you play online, your moves, your team and outfit and a random session ID are relayed live to your opponent through Supabase Realtime. Nothing is stored after the match and no name or account is involved.

Bug reports

If you send a bug report from Settings, your description, the e-mail address you entered (optional) and technical details (app platform and version, device and browser model, screen size, language and basic game progress) are sent by e-mail to the developer through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co). We use them only to fix the problem and to reply to you, and delete them on request.

Web portal versions

When you play on a web portal (for example via Playgama), the portal shows ads, processes payments and may collect usage statistics under its own privacy policy. Bug reports there go to the portal’s game statistics without an e-mail address.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads for younger players can be limited through Google’s consent tools.

Your rights

Deleting the Game deletes all data it stored on your device. To delete a bug report you sent, or for help with data held by Google or RevenueCat, e-mail dynamo1994@gmail.com and we will handle it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко)

Игра хранит прогресс только на вашем устройстве, аккаунт не нужен, своих серверов у нас нет. Реклама показывается через Google AdMob: используются рекламный идентификатор устройства (на iPhone только с вашего разрешения в запросе «Разрешить отслеживание»), IP, диагностика и данные о взаимодействии с рекламой. Покупки обрабатывают Apple и Google Play, проверку покупок делает RevenueCat (анонимный ID и история покупок). В онлайн-боях ходы и случайный ID сессии передаются сопернику через Supabase Realtime и не сохраняются. Отчёт об ошибке из настроек (текст, e-mail по желанию и данные об устройстве) приходит разработчику на почту через FormSubmit. Изменить согласие на рекламу: Язык → Настройки рекламы и приватности. Вопросы и удаление данных: dynamo1994@gmail.com.
