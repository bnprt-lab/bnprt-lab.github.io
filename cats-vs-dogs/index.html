FAQ

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open Premium (👑) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Cats vs Dogs → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I change ad tracking / consent?

iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA/UK also: in the game, Language → Ad privacy settings.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on the device. Deleting the app deletes it; purchases can always be restored.
